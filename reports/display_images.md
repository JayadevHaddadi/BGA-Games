# Game page "display" images: rule and audit

## Rule (all agents)

The **display images** in the Game Metadata Manager (GMM) must be **photos of the real, physical game**:
box, board, pieces, a game in progress on a table. They must NOT be:

- screenshots of our BGA implementation,
- print-and-play templates or rulebook diagrams,
- generated or AI art, placeholder or blank images.

Upload **at least 2 and at most 10**; more is better. Repo copies live in
`games/<slug>/bga/metadata_assets/display_<n>.jpg` (1000x750 or similar landscape JPG).

**Where photos come from:** the publisher (for nestorgames: Néstor, info@nestorgames.com) or the
publisher's own product pages. Néstor already sent a People photo pack (mail "People pics", 2026-10-22,
`_People.zip`). Do NOT take user-uploaded BoardGameGeek photos: each one belongs to the person who
took it, and BGA asks the publisher about asset rights (AI Act declaration). Photos that the
publisher itself uploaded to BGG are fine once the publisher confirms.
