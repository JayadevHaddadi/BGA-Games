## Description (paste)

Hippos & Crocodiles is a tactical abstract strategy game for 2 players by Néstor Romeral Andrés. Players take turns placing their species of animal (Hippos or Crocodiles, each taking up 10 squares on the grid) into the river or lake. When a player cannot place a piece on their turn, they lose immediately; the last player to place a piece wins.
