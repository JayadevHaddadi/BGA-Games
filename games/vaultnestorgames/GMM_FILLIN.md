## Description (paste)

Vault is a 2-player abstract strategy game designed by Néstor Romeral Andrés and published by nestorgames in 2015. Played on a 10x10 four-toned checkered board, each player commands 10 pawns starting on opposite sides of the grid. On their turn, a player must either walk a pawn one step in any direction to an adjacent empty space, or jump one pawn over another friendly pawn along any straight vector—doubling the distance beyond the pivot pawn and capturing any enemy pawn occupying the landing square.

The objective is to vault across the board and establish a pawn on the opponent's back rank. If you begin your turn with at least one pawn on the opponent's back rank, or if your opponent has no legal moves remaining on their turn, you win.
