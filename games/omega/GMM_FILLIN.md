## Description (paste)

Omega is a combinatorial abstract strategy game for 2 to 4 players played on a hexagonal board of hexagonal cells.

On your turn, you place one stone of each color in play onto empty cells of the board. The game ends when a full round cannot be completed. At game end, each player's score is calculated by multiplying the sizes of all connected groups of stones of their color. The player with the highest score wins.
