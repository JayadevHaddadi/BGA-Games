## Description (paste)

A dystopian city where people are the crop. Build factories between the districts of the game area: each factory scores the humans on its two sides, multiplied together. Use the police to disperse the humans, the bulldozer to sweep them along the streets, roadblocks to cut the city off, and more people to fill it up. Spend your bitcoins wisely: unspent ones count as points at the end.

A tribute to Soylent Green. The game is for 2 to 4 players. The game area shrinks as the table fills, and the game ends when the player to move has no factory left to build.
