\section{The Idea}
Six kids, six toy armies. Each commander leads cute little \textbf{Bots} and a few huge \textbf{Mechs} across a floor of hexagon tiles. Dig up iron and crystal, build factories, grab land, and complete \textbf{Missions}. The first commander to reach \textbf{5 Victory Points} wins.

\section{Components}
\begin{itemize}
\item Land tiles: 37 (centre $+$ 3 rings) and 6 Home tiles. Sea tiles: 12 Port tiles.
\item 6 Player boards (one per faction) and 1 Market board.
\item Per player: 20 Bots, 6 Mechs, 5 Extractors, 5 Factories, 5 Guard Towers, 1 Dock.
\item Tokens: Iron, Crystal, Credit coins (1, 5, 10), price markers, VP markers.
\item 40 Mission cards (20 of Level~1, 20 of Level~2).
\end{itemize}

\section{Setup}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Board.} 2--3 players use 19 land tiles (centre and 2 rings). 4--6 players use 37 land tiles (centre and 3 rings). Within each ring the tiles are shuffled and placed at random. Roads must line up only where \emph{both} touching tiles show one.
\item \textbf{Ports.} Place 2 Port tiles per player just outside the land. Each Port joins by road to the 2 land tiles beside it.
\item \textbf{Homes.} Spread the Home tiles evenly around the outer ring. Each player takes one, with a Dock and a Guard Tower on it, \textbf{2 Iron} and \textbf{3 Bots}. Build your first Extractor with that Iron.
\item \textbf{Market.} Set the four price markers (Iron, Bots, Crystal, Mechs) at random in their starting band: Iron 4--7, Bots 4--7, Crystal 8--14, Mechs 8--14.
\item \textbf{Missions.} Shuffle 10 random Level~1 cards and 10 random Level~2 cards. Level~1 cards go on top. Reveal 5.
\item The first player starts with 10 Credits. Everyone else receives their 10 Credits at the start of their first turn, like all income.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Your Turn}
There are no rounds. When your turn starts, you receive \textbf{+10 Credits} and all your Extractors refresh. Then do any of the actions below, in any order and as often as you can pay for. End your turn when done; your attacks resolve then.

\subsection{Control}
You \textbf{control} a tile while at least one of your pieces is on it. Every building and token on a tile belongs to whoever controls the tile. Bots cannot be assigned to buildings. They only hold tiles and attack.

\subsection{Build}
Pay iron that is standing on the tile, and use a free building area there:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{0.8\textwidth}{lXl}
\toprule
Building & What it does & Cost \\
\midrule
Extractor & Once per turn, pay 1 Credit to take 1 token of the tile's resource & 1 Iron \\
Factory & 1 Iron $\to$ 2 Bots, or 1 Iron $+$ 1 Crystal $\to$ 1 Mech & 2 Iron \\
Guard Tower & Makes the tile harder to push and kill (see Combat) & 2 Iron \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
An Extractor needs a tile with a resource. A tile has 0, 1 or 2 building areas. You can sell a building: it returns to your board and you get its cost minus 1 Iron back onto the tile.

\subsection{Make Pieces}
At a Factory, spend its inputs from the same tile. New Bots and Mechs appear on that tile. They come from your player board's supply (20 Bots, 6 Mechs).

\subsection{Move}
Each piece pays the \textbf{triangular cost} of its \emph{total} steps this turn: 1 step costs 1, 2 steps cost 3, 3 steps cost 6, 4 steps cost 10. Credits already paid for that piece this turn count toward the new total, so a piece that has moved can keep going and only pays the difference. Pieces follow open roads and cannot pass through enemy pieces.

\subsection{Trade}
\begin{itemize}
\item Trade from a tile you control that has a Dock or a Port.
\item Your home Dock trades \textbf{Iron} and \textbf{Bots} at the current market price.
\item A Port is a separate tile outside the land. Each Port buys one good for 2 Credits less, or pays 3 Credits more when you sell it.
\item You choose a quantity. Every item trades at the current price, then the price moves one step per item traded (up when bought, down when sold). A step is 1 Credit for Iron and Bots, 2 Credits for Crystal and Mechs. Prices only move when somebody trades.
\item Price ranges: Iron 1--10, Bots 1--10, Crystal 2--20, Mechs 2--20.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Missions}
Pay \textbf{5 Credits} to buy a face-up Mission, plus 1 more for every Mission you have already bought. You may buy one per turn. If you meet its condition \emph{at that moment} you score its VP (1 for Level~1, 2 for Level~2). If you do not, the Credits are lost. A completed card is replaced from the deck.

\subsection{Undo}
Undo and Undo all (digital version) take back your actions in the current turn. They disappear when the turn ends.

\section{Combat}
Move pieces onto an enemy tile to attack it. Attackers walk in; the attack is decided when you end your turn.
\begin{itemize}
\item A Bot has power \textbf{1}. A Mech has power \textbf{4}.
\item Add up the attackers' power and the defenders' power.
\item \textbf{Push:} the attackers' total is at least \textbf{2$\times$} the defenders'. The defenders must retreat. The defending player chooses the tile each pushed piece retreats to.
\item \textbf{Kill:} the attackers' total is at least \textbf{3$\times$} the defenders'. The defenders are removed.
\item Each Guard Tower on the tile adds 1 to both multipliers (push $2\to3\to\dots$, kill $3\to4\to\dots$).
\item If defenders remain, it is an \textbf{attack failure}: the attackers return to where they came from.
\end{itemize}

\section{Winning}
The first player to reach \textbf{5 Victory Points} wins immediately. Victory Points come only from Missions.
