Mission cards (40)

Level 1 = 1 VP, Level 2 = 2 VP. A game uses 10 random cards per level; 5 face up. Buy cost 5 Credits (+1 per card you already bought); VP only if the condition is met at that moment.

Known issue: "Rich Land" cards (L1-16, L2-19) need hexes with two resources, but the current board generator never creates one (resource_type_2 is always empty), so they cannot be completed yet. Either add double-resource tiles or replace these two cards.
