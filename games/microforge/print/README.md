# Print and play (2 players, black and white, A4)

`little_commanders_print_2p.pdf` (15 pages) is generated, do not edit it. Rebuild after any rule or art change:

    python3 design/build_design.py && python3 design/print_sheets.py

Print at 100% ("actual size"). Pages: 1 how-to and setup, 2-3 hexagon tiles (21 land, 8 port) and a setup map, 4-5 bots, mechs,
buildings, docks, price markers, Credit coins, iron, crystal, VP, 6-7 market board (Basic side, Advanced side), 8-11 player boards
(2 players x Basic/Advanced), 12-15 mission cards (36: Guard Tower and centre-hex cards are left out, they cannot be played).
Hexagons touch edge to edge (shared cuts); everything else is a square or rectangle in a tight grid. Player 1 = triangle, player 2 = circle + grey.
