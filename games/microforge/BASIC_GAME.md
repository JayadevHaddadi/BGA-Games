# Basic game (draft proposal)

**Aim:** teachable in a minute, still has a goal, tension and choices. Everything else is printed on the board, tiles and cards.

## Rules in your head (two)
1. **Credits:** you earn 1 Credit per tile you hold and spend them: recruit a Bot at home (2), move a piece one step (1 per step), buy a Mission (5, +1 each time you buy).
2. **Strength:** move onto an enemy tile with more Bots than the defenders and they retreat. Otherwise you go back.

## Goal
First to 3 Victory Points. VP come only from Level 1 Mission cards (5 face up, shared), bought for Credits and scored only if you meet them when you buy. For the Basic game use the cards that need only Bots and tiles: hexes 3/4/5, bots 5/7, pieces 6, centre, ports 1/2, credits 15.

## Why it is not trivial (strategies)
- **Land or army?** Tiles give income, Bots give safety. Over-expand and you are cut off; sit still and you fall behind.
- **Chokepoints:** tiles with few roads (broken stumps) are easy to defend. The centre has six roads: rich in options but hard to hold.
- **Mission race:** five cards are shared. Taking one the others also need denies them, but buying a card you do not meet wastes 5+ Credits, so timing and bluffing matter.
- **Rising mission price:** the first card is cheap; each later one costs +1, so snowballing is limited.
- **Stack size reads:** pieces are public, so players bluff by splitting or merging stacks and by keeping a reserve at home.
- **Counter-raids:** emptying your home tile to attack invites a counter-raid on the tile you left.

## Advanced layers (add one at a time)
1. **Production:** iron/crystal tokens, Extractor, Factory, Guard Tower.
2. **Mechs and combat multipliers** (push x2, kill x3).
3. **Market and ports** (see below).
4. **Faction powers.**

## Market options (to shrink the market)
- **A. Fixed ports:** no moving prices at all; each port prints its own buy/sell price. Simplest, no arbitrage.
- **B. One moving price:** only Iron has a price track that moves per item traded; ports add a fixed printed bonus.
- **C. Today:** four moving tracks (Iron, Bots, Crystal, Mechs).
Recommendation: B for the Advanced game.
