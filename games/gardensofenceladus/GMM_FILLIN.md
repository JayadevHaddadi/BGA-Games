## Description (paste)

Our Martians from Gardens of Mars and Gardens of Io have travelled to Saturn's moon Enceladus to harvest the flowers they planted last year. Your little Martian gardener moves across an icy garden of 10 x 10 squares, collecting flowers of five colours from the board. Every flower with no neighbour is gathered at once, and special actions let you swap and move peaks and flowers around the garden.

At the end of the game each player's reserve is read as a hand: the number of flowers of each colour, sorted from lowest to highest. The best hand wins. Balance your colours, because a hand with a missing colour is terribly low. A cooperative variant asks for the highest team score you can reach.
